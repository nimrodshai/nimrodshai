    Our process

    Slow on purpose.

    Commercial yeast would get us there in three hours. We take two days — because time is the ingredient that turns flour and water into something that tastes of wheat, tang and caramel.

    1. 01

      Feed the starter

      Our starter, “Shimshon,” has lived on the counter since 2019. He gets rye flour and spring water twice a day, and bubbles like he means it.

      Day 1 · 8pm
    2. 02

      Stretch & fold

      No machines. Every 40 minutes we lift and fold the dough by hand, building strength and those big, glossy, irregular holes.

      Day 2 · 6am–11am
    3. 03

      Cold proof

      Shaped loaves rest overnight in linen-lined baskets at 4°C. The slow chill deepens the flavour and gives the crust its blistered freckles.

      Day 2 · overnight
    4. 04

      Bake hot & dark

      Into a stone deck oven at 250°C with a burst of steam. We pull them late — a dark crust means flavour, and you can hear it crackle as it cools.

      Day 3 · 5am
