Our story

A stone house, a single fire, and nothing to hide behind.

We opened Olive & Ember in 2019 inside a vaulted house two lanes up from the Jaffa port — a former soap workshop whose walls still hold the cool of the sea at night. We built the oven by hand that first winter, brick by brick, with a mason from Akko who refused to let us rush the curve of the dome.

Everything on the menu passes through that fire. Fish arrives from the port before noon. Vegetables come from two small farms in the Sharon and the Jezreel Valley. Olive oil is pressed for us each November in the Galilee. When something isn't at its best, it simply isn't on the menu.
