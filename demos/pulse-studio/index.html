01 — The Work

Four ways
to empty the tank

Every class is capped at 22 people, coached on the floor (not from a stage), and programmed in 6-week blocks so you actually get stronger.

01 45 min

HIIT

Intervals that hit like a drop. Sled pushes, ropes, rowers and burpees on a clock that doesn't negotiate. Burns for 24 hours after.

02 50 min

Ride

Rhythm cycling in the dark. Climbs, sprints and a playlist built by a DJ, not an algorithm. Leaderboard optional, sweat mandatory.

03 55 min

Strength

Barbells, kettlebells, real progressive overload. Coaches track your numbers so every block you lift a little heavier than last time.

04 60 min

Mobility

Yoga-rooted flow, deep stretch and breathwork. The class your hamstrings have been begging for — and the reason you'll still be training at 60.
