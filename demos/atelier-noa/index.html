03

The studio

“A good room does not ask to be looked at. It waits, and then — on a Tuesday in November, at four in the afternoon — it gives you something.”

Atelier Noa is a studio of six: two architects, two interior designers, a project manager and a model maker who refuses to use the laser cutter. We take on a handful of projects each year so that every one of them gets the founder's own hand, from the first sketch to the final walk-through.

We work with what the Mediterranean already knows — thick walls, deep reveals, shade as a material — and with craftspeople we have known for a decade: stonemasons in the Galilee, a joinery in Kfar Saba, a lime plasterer from Akko.

04

What we do

Every commission begins with a site visit and a long conversation. From there, a clear scope and a fixed fee.

  1. Private houses, additions and rooftop extensions, from feasibility and permits through construction documents and site supervision. We manage the engineers, so you don't have to.

    • Feasibility & zoning study
    • Concept & permit design
    • Construction drawings
    • Site supervision

  2. Tel Aviv's 1930s–70s apartments are our home ground. We restore what is worth keeping — terrazzo, steel windows, ribbon balconies — and rethink the plan around how you actually live.

    • Measured survey
    • Layout & structural changes
    • Conservation liaison
    • Turnkey delivery

  3. Material palettes, custom joinery, lighting design and the last ten percent — the furniture, linen and objects that make a space feel inhabited rather than finished.

    • Material & colour palettes
    • Bespoke joinery
    • Lighting design
    • Furniture & styling

  4. Hotels of eight to forty keys, designed with operators from day one: guest flow, housekeeping logic and durable materials that age into character rather than out of it.

    • Brand & guest journey
    • Room typologies
    • FF&E procurement
    • Opening support
