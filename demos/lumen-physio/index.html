What we treat

Care for every stage of recovery

From a first twinge to a full return to sport — every programme starts with a thorough assessment and is adjusted as you progress.

  • Sports injuries

    Sprains, tendon pain, ACL and running injuries — rehab that gets you back to the game stronger than before.

    45 min · 1:1

  • Back & neck pain

    Manual therapy, movement retraining and practical ergonomics for long days at a desk or behind the wheel.

    45 min · 1:1

  • Post-surgery rehab

    Structured recovery after knee, hip, shoulder and spine surgery, coordinated closely with your surgeon.

    45–60 min · 1:1

  • Pelvic floor

    Discreet, specialist care for pregnancy, postpartum recovery, continence and pelvic pain — in a private room.

    50 min · private room

  • Hydrotherapy

    Gentle exercise in our 34°C therapy pool — ideal for arthritis, early post-op stages and chronic pain.

    40 min · warm pool

  • Kids' physio

    Playful, family-centred sessions for motor development, posture, sports injuries and coordination.

    40 min · ages 2–16

How it works

Three calm steps back to movement

  1. 01

    In-depth assessment

    A 60-minute first visit: your history, goals and a full movement analysis. You'll leave understanding exactly what's going on.

  2. 02

    Your personal plan

    Hands-on treatment plus a short home programme sent to your phone, with video demos you can follow anywhere.

  3. 03

    Recover & return

    We measure progress every session and taper visits as you gain strength — the goal is independence, not dependence.
